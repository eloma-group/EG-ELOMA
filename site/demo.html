Why it matters

One system instead of a stack of subscriptions

Many restaurants pay separately for an online ordering platform, a point-of-sale system, a kitchen display, a phone system and delivery-app tablets. Each has its own monthly fee, login and copy of the data.

This demo shows the alternative: one system, built around how the business works, that handles every order from every channel. Because the business owns it, there are no per-user licence fees, and any developer can maintain it.

The same approach works for other industries, from trades and clinics to retail and professional services.

FAQ

Demo questions

Is this a real system?

It's an interactive prototype with sample data, built to show how one owned system can run a whole business. No real orders, calls or payments are made.

Can you build something like this for my business?

Yes. We design each system around how your business works, whether that's a restaurant, a trade business, a clinic or a retailer. Book a free consultation to talk it through.

Would we own the software?

Yes. You own the system, the source code and your data, so there are no per-user licence fees.

Can it connect to delivery apps and payment providers?

We can integrate with providers that offer access for partners and businesses, such as payment gateways and ordering platforms. We'll confirm what's possible for your setup.

Does my data stay private in the demo?

Yes. The demo runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to us.
