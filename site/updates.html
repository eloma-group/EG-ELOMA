Still using Windows 10? Plan your upgrade now

Microsoft ended support for Windows 10 on 14 October 2025, so those computers no longer get free security updates. Microsoft 365 apps still get security updates on Windows 10 until October 2028, but the computer itself is exposed.

What to do: Check which computers still run Windows 10 and plan an upgrade or replacement. We can check this for you.

Book a free checkSource: Microsoft
Security advisoryRecommended5 Nov 2026

Watch out for fake Microsoft 365 sign-in emails

Scam emails that look like Microsoft 365 messages ("your mailbox is full", "shared document", "password expires") try to steal staff passwords. They often use real Microsoft logos and urgent wording.

What to do: Remind staff never to sign in from an email link, and make sure multi-factor authentication is on for every account.

Our security servicesSource: Scamwatch

Essential Eight: where small businesses should start

The ASD's Essential Eight is a set of eight security strategies. For most small businesses the best first steps are multi-factor authentication, keeping software patched and regular backups you've tested.

What to do: Ask us for a free Essential Eight check to see where your business stands.

How we handle Microsoft's monthly security updates

Microsoft releases security updates on the second Tuesday of each month. For clients on our support plans, we test and roll them out to managed computers and servers, and follow up on any urgent fixes.

What to do: No action needed if you're on an EG Eloma support plan. Otherwise, make sure automatic updates are switched on.

FAQ

About our updates

Who are these updates for?

Everyone. Clients on our support plans also get updates that apply to their own systems directly, by email or in the client portal.

How often do you post updates?

Whenever something affects our clients, and at least once a month with a summary of Microsoft and security changes.

Where does the information come from?

Official sources such as Microsoft and the Australian Signals Directorate (cyber.gov.au), explained in our own words. Each update links to its source.

Do I need to act on every update?

No. Each update is labelled Action required, Recommended or For information, and tells you exactly what to do. If you're unsure, ask us.
