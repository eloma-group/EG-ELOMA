Overview

Google & Meta Ads, explained

Paid ads on Google, Facebook and Instagram can bring in customers quickly, but only if they're set up well. Many businesses spend money on ads that reach the wrong people or can't be measured.

We build campaigns around your goals, track the enquiries and sales they produce, and keep testing to improve results. Your ad spend is always shown separately from our fee, so you know exactly where your money goes.

Who it's for

  • Businesses wanting enquiries quickly
  • Businesses launching a new product or location
  • Owners unsure whether their current ads work
  • eCommerce stores wanting more sales

Why it matters

Why Google & Meta Ads matters for your business

Paid ads put your business in front of people right now, whether they're searching on Google or scrolling Facebook and Instagram. That makes them ideal for fast enquiries, launches and seasonal offers.

But ads can waste money quickly. Broad targeting, weak ads and missing tracking mean you pay for clicks that never become customers.

We set up campaigns around your real goals, track every enquiry, and keep improving. You see exactly what you spend and what you get back.

FAQ

Google & Meta Ads questions

How much should we spend on ads?

It depends on your market and goals. We recommend a starting budget in your quote and show ad spend separately.

Which plan includes ads?

The All-in-One plan, or the Ad Power single-service plan.

How quickly will we see results?

Ads can start bringing visitors as soon as they go live. Performance usually improves over the first weeks as we test and optimise.

Do we own the ad accounts?

Yes. Campaigns run in accounts you own.

Can you take over our existing campaigns?

Yes. We review them first and tell you what we'd keep and change.

Which is better, Google or Meta?

Google captures people already searching; Meta builds demand and awareness. Many businesses use both.

Do you create the ad images and videos?

Yes, through our design team.

Can we pause campaigns any time?

Yes. You stay in control of your budget.
