Overview

SEO, explained

Search engine optimisation is how your business shows up when customers search for what you sell. Unlike ads, good rankings keep bringing visitors without paying for every click.

We start by finding the searches that bring in customers, not just traffic. Then we fix technical issues, improve your pages, build your authority and report on results every month in plain English. One client moved from position 13 to number 1 on Google for their main search term in six months.

Who it's for

  • Businesses that rely on Google to find customers
  • Sites that rank on page two or lower
  • Businesses entering new markets or countries
  • Owners spending heavily on ads and wanting another source of leads

Why it matters

Why SEO matters for your business

When someone searches for what you sell, the businesses on the first page get almost all the clicks. If you're on page two, most potential customers never see you.

SEO is a long-term investment. Unlike ads, which stop when you stop paying, strong rankings keep bringing visitors month after month. Over time, good SEO can lower your cost per enquiry significantly.

Good SEO isn't tricks. It's making your site fast and easy for Google to understand, answering the questions your customers ask, and earning a reputation that other websites link to.
