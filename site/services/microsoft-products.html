FAQ

Microsoft Products questions

How long does a rollout take?

A typical Microsoft rollout takes about 90 days, from consultation to handover.

Can you migrate us from another email system?

Yes. Migration is part of the rollout.

Will email go down during migration?

We plan migrations to keep disruption to a minimum, usually switching over outside business hours.

Is Copilot safe with our data?

We review permissions first, so Copilot only sees what each person should see.

Do you support Microsoft 365 after setup?

Yes, with a 24/7 helpdesk.

Can you set up shared mailboxes and calendars?

Yes, along with groups, permissions and distribution lists.

Do you manage devices too?

Yes. We can manage and secure laptops and phones through Microsoft tools.

Can you migrate from Google Workspace?

Yes. We plan and run migrations from Google Workspace and other systems.
