Overview

Local Listing, explained

When someone searches for a service near them, Google shows a map with local businesses. Your Google Business Profile decides whether you appear, and whether people choose you.

We set up and verify your profile, fill in every detail, add photos and posts, and help you collect and reply to reviews. We also keep your business details consistent across other directories, which helps Google trust your listing.

Who it's for

  • Businesses that serve a local area
  • Shops, clinics, trades and service businesses
  • Businesses with an incomplete or unverified profile
  • Businesses with few or outdated reviews

Why it matters

Why Local Listing matters for your business

For local businesses, Google Maps is often where customers decide. People search 'near me', look at the map results, compare reviews and call or get directions.

Your Google Business Profile drives this. A complete profile with the right categories, photos, posts and reviews is far more likely to appear and be chosen.

We set up and manage your profile and local listings so nearby customers find you first, and we help you build a steady flow of reviews.

FAQ

Local Listing questions

Do we need a physical address?

Yes. Google requires a real business location or service area. We don't use virtual offices.

Which plan includes this?

Growth, Business and All-in-One plans, or on its own as Near Me.

We don't have a shopfront. Can we still have a profile?

Yes, as a service-area business, as long as you have a real business address. We don't use virtual offices.

Can you remove bad reviews?

Google only removes reviews that break its policies. We help you respond professionally and earn more positive reviews.

How long until we appear on Maps?

It depends on competition and verification. We report progress monthly.

Can you manage profiles for several locations?

Yes. We can manage each location's profile.

Do reviews really affect ranking?

Reviews and how you respond to them are among the signals that influence local results.

Can you add booking or ordering buttons?

Where your booking or ordering system supports it, yes.
