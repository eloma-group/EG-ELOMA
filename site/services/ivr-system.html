Overview

IVR System, explained

When every call lands with one person, customers wait and calls get passed around. A phone menu sends callers to the right person first time.

We design your IVR menu, record professional greetings and set rules for business hours, holidays and busy times, so every caller is handled properly.

Who it's for

  • Businesses with several departments
  • Teams handling high call volumes
  • Businesses taking after-hours calls
  • Anyone wanting a more professional phone experience

FAQ

IVR System questions

Can we change the menu ourselves?

We make changes for you quickly, and can set up simple options you control.

Does it work with toll-free numbers?

Yes. IVR works with toll-free, landline and mobile numbers.

How many menu options should we have?

Fewer is usually better. We'll recommend a simple menu that callers can follow easily.

Can we update greetings for holidays?

Yes. We update them for you.

Can the IVR send calls to virtual customer care?

Yes. Calls can overflow to our customer care team.

Can callers leave messages for each team?

Yes, with separate voicemail per team.

Can the menu change by time of day?

Yes, with business hours and holiday rules.

Do you record greetings?

Yes, professional greetings can be recorded for you.
