Overview

Microsoft for Nonprofits, explained

Charities and nonprofits need reliable, secure technology, but every dollar spent on IT is a dollar not spent on your mission. Microsoft offers donated and discounted products for eligible organisations.

We check whether you qualify, help apply the offers, then set up Microsoft 365 and Azure properly with security, file sharing and training. Microsoft sets eligibility and the offers available, which can change over time.

Who it's for

  • Registered Australian charities
  • Nonprofits paying full price for Microsoft 365
  • Organisations with volunteers and staff sharing files
  • Nonprofits wanting to use Azure credits

Why Microsoft for Nonprofits matters for your business

Nonprofits run on tight budgets, and technology costs can take money away from the mission. Microsoft's nonprofit offers can make a real difference for eligible organisations.

Getting the offers is only half the job. Volunteers and staff need secure accounts, organised files and simple tools that work wherever they are.

FAQ

Microsoft for Nonprofits questions

Who is eligible?

Microsoft sets eligibility. Generally, registered charities and nonprofits qualify. We'll check yours for free.

What offers are available?

Eligible Australian charities may get Microsoft 365 Business Basic free for up to 300 users, discounted Business Premium and an annual Azure credit grant. Microsoft sets and may change these offers.

Is the eligibility check free?

Yes, there's no charge to check.

Do you support nonprofits outside Australia?

Our nonprofit licensing service is for Australian organisations. We can advise others on setup.

What does setup cost?

Setup and support are quoted in writing after a free consultation.

Can volunteers have accounts?

Yes. We can set up accounts appropriate to each volunteer's role.

Can you help with donated Azure credits?

Yes. We can set up useful services using your credits.

Do the offers ever change?

Yes. Microsoft sets and updates its nonprofit offers. We keep you informed.
