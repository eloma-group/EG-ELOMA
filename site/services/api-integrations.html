Overview

API Integrations, explained

When systems don't talk to each other, people fill the gap by copying data between them. That wastes time and creates errors in invoices, orders and reports.

We connect the tools you rely on so information moves automatically: a sale in your store creates an invoice, a new lead appears in your CRM, a booking updates your calendar. Integrations can also be the first step towards one owned system.

Who it's for

  • Teams copying data between apps every day
  • Businesses with errors in orders or invoices
  • Companies using Microsoft 365 alongside other tools
  • Anyone planning to move to one system in stages

Why it matters

Why API Integrations matters for your business

Most businesses run on several systems: accounting, CRM, eCommerce, payments, email and more. When they don't talk to each other, people become the connection, copying data from one screen to another.

That manual work is slow and error-prone. A wrong number in an invoice or a missed order costs time and money, and it often goes unnoticed until a customer complains.

Integrations let your systems share information automatically and reliably. They're also a sensible first step towards one owned system, connecting what you have today while you plan what comes next.

FAQ

API Integrations questions

Which systems can you connect?

Most systems with an API, including common CRM, accounting, eCommerce and payment platforms, plus Microsoft 365.

What happens if a connection fails?

We set up monitoring and alerts so issues are caught and fixed quickly.

What if a system has no API?

We look at other options such as file exports or scheduled imports, and tell you honestly what's possible.

Are integrations secure?

Yes. We use secure authentication and only share the data that's needed.

Who looks after integrations once they're built?

We can monitor and maintain them, or hand over documentation to your team.

Can you replace tools like Zapier?

Often, yes. Custom integrations can be more reliable and cheaper at higher volumes.

How long do integrations take to build?

It depends on the systems involved. We include a timeline in your quote.

Will we be charged per connection or per task?

No. Custom integrations are quoted in writing, without per-task fees.
