Overview

App Development, explained

A mobile app puts your business in your customers' pockets, or gives your team the tools they need in the field. Done well, it saves time and keeps customers coming back.

We build apps for iOS and Android, usually from one cross-platform codebase to keep cost down. Apps connect to your existing systems, so information flows between the app, your office and your customers.

Who it's for

  • Businesses with staff working on the road or on site
  • Businesses whose customers book or order often
  • Companies wanting a branded app for loyalty or service
  • Anyone extending custom software to mobile

Why it matters

Why App Development matters for your business

Phones are where your customers and field staff spend their day. An app can make booking, ordering or reporting from site faster than any website or paper form.

Apps work best when they're connected. A staff app that updates jobs in your office system, or a customer app that shows real order history, is far more useful than a standalone tool.

We build iOS and Android apps from one cross-platform codebase to keep cost and maintenance down, and connect them to the systems you already rely on.

FAQ

App Development questions

Do we need separate apps for iPhone and Android?

Usually not. A cross-platform build covers both from one codebase.

Can the app work with our existing system?

Yes. We connect it through APIs to the systems you already use.

Can the app work without internet?

Yes, we can design key features to work offline and sync when connected.

Who manages the App Store listings?

We publish under your developer accounts, so you own the listings.

Can you update the app after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing updates and support.

How long does App Store approval take?

Apple and Google review apps before publishing. We prepare everything to their guidelines to keep approval smooth.

Can you update an existing app?

Yes. We can review your current app and improve or rebuild it.

Do apps need ongoing maintenance?

Yes. Phone operating systems update regularly, so apps need occasional updates to keep working well.
